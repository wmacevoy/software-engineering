## Extraction

Reprint page 329. Figure 2 occupies the top of the page. Its caption:

> Figure 2. Implementation steps to develop a large computer program for delivery to a customer.

The first sentence of the paragraph directly beneath the caption:

> I believe in this concept, but the implementation described above is risky and invites failure.

The paragraph continues: "The testing phase which occurs at the end of the development cycle is the
first event for which timing, storage, input/output transfers, etc., are experienced as
distinguished from analyzed." The page's last paragraph: "However, I believe the illustrated
approach to be fundamentally sound." Page 330 holds Figures 3 and 4: the iteration hoped to be
confined to successive steps, and the iterations that never are.
